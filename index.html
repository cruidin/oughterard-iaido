About

Iaido (居合道) is a traditional Japanese martial art centred on the drawing of the sword, responding to a sudden attack, and returning the blade to its scabbard. There is no sparring — the discipline lies entirely in precision, timing, and stillness.

Training consists of a set sequence of forms (kata), practised individually and slowly until the movement becomes quiet and exact. This group trains in the style Muso Jikiden Eishin Ryu, and is affiliated with Kenseikai Ireland, the Dublin dojo led by Sensei Nick Johnston.
