What is Iaido

The art of the single, decisive cut.

Iaido (居合道) is a Japanese martial art centred on the smooth, controlled drawing of the sword, responding to a sudden attack, and returning the blade to its scabbard with equal composure. There is no sparring — the discipline is entirely in precision, timing, and stillness.

Training is done individually, through a sequence of forms (kata), practised slowly and deliberately until the movement becomes quiet and exact. It suits patient, self-directed practice as much as group training.
